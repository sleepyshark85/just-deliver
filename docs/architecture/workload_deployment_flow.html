Workload deployment flow

Resolution is a pure, backend-agnostic step that emits a graph with open references. A gate inspects the graph before anything runs, then a durable orchestrator executes it one queued step at a time.

Inside the orchestrator

Step order from A1 / D17 / E28. Each step is one queue round trip, checkpointed before the next, so a restart resumes rather than starts over.

Changed from the sketch

What moved and why

  • Parse → resolved graph, not finished yml. Open refs are filled per step (C52 phases, D17).
  • Definition parser left the Backend-provider frame. It is now the backend-agnostic resolver.
  • Definition mappings are versioned data, alongside IT Ops policies and the environment.
  • Preview & approve gate added between resolve and deploy (A2 diff, C11 replace check).
  • Single provider call became a durable step loop through a session queue (E29, E30).
  • Failure and degraded end states added; the sketch only had “Done” (E31, A1 rider 2).

Still open

Needs a decision

  • Where the orchestrator checkpoints state, and how a retry re-enters (E29, E31).
